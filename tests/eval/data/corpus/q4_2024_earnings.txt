IntraMind Q4 2024 Earnings Summary

Total revenue for Q4 2024 was 12.4 million dollars, up 18 percent year over year.
Operating expenses came in at 8.1 million dollars. Net income was 3.2 million
dollars, exceeding the analyst consensus of 2.9 million dollars.

The North America region contributed 7.5 million dollars in revenue, while EMEA
contributed 3.1 million dollars and APAC contributed 1.8 million dollars.

The board of directors approved a quarterly dividend of 0.15 dollars per share,
payable on March 15, 2025.
