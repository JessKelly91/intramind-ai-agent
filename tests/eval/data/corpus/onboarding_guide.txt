Onboarding Guide for New Engineers

New engineers should expect a two-week onboarding program. Week one focuses on
environment setup, security training, and codebase orientation. Week two
focuses on shipping a small starter task with a designated buddy.

All new engineers receive a MacBook Pro with 32 GB of RAM by default. Requests
for additional hardware should be filed through the IT portal.

The first 1:1 with the engineering manager happens on day three. Buddies are
assigned by the team lead within 48 hours of the engineer's start date.
