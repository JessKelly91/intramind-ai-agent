Acceptable Use Policy

All employees must use company systems in accordance with this Acceptable Use
Policy. The policy applies to laptops, mobile devices, and any cloud services
provisioned by IT.

Prohibited activities include sharing credentials, installing unapproved
software, and storing customer data on personal devices. Violations may result
in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Employees should report suspected violations to the security team within 24
hours of discovery.
